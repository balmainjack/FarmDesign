🌾 Design Your Own Sustainable Farm

Year 9 Geography · Sustainable Biomes · NSW

1. Name your farm

You've inherited a block of land. Give it a name and tell us who's running it.

2. Choose your region

Pick where your farm is. Climate and rainfall will shape what you can grow.

🔍 Justify your region choice

Before you move on, do a quick search and write 1–2 sentences explaining why this region's climate, rainfall and soil make it suitable for farming.

  • What is the average annual rainfall in your region?
  • What soil types are common there?
  • What agricultural industries are already established in this region?
Suggested sources: Bureau of Meteorology (bom.gov.au), GRDC Grower Regions, NSW DPI, your textbook.

3. Choose your produce

Options that don't suit your region are dimmed. You can still pick them — but read the warning.

🔍 Research your crop

Find three facts about your chosen crop and explain why it suits (or struggles with) your region.

  • What growing conditions does this crop need (rainfall, temperature, soil)?
  • How long is the growing season?
  • What is the biggest environmental risk to this crop in your region?
Suggested sources: GRDC, MLA, NSW DPI, CSIRO, AgriFutures.

4. Face the challenge

Every biome pushes back. What's the biggest environmental threat to your farm?

🔍 Research your challenge

Use a source to find out how this challenge affects farms in your region and what farmers are already doing about it.

  • How does this challenge reduce farm productivity?
  • Is this challenge getting worse due to climate change?
  • What solutions have farmers already tried in your region?
Suggested sources: NSW DPI, CSIRO, Bureau of Meteorology, Landcare Australia.

5. Choose your sustainable fix

How will you farm sustainably and reduce the problem you identified?

🔍 Research your strategy

Find evidence that this strategy works. Look for a case study, statistic or example of a real Australian farm using it.

  • How much does this strategy reduce the problem (e.g. "cuts water use by X%")?
  • What is the upfront cost, and does it save money long-term?
  • Find one real Australian farm or region using this strategy.
Suggested sources: Landcare Australia, GRDC, MLA, NSW DPI, CSIRO, Farmers for Climate Action.

6. Three seasons of chance

A farm isn't judged on one season — it's judged on three. Roll once per season to see how your strategy holds up.
